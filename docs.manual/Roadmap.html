    EnhancementLaterRequested by: Tukwut
  • Elemental swarm arena: waves of 24 elementals trickle-spawned to surround the party, the element rotating each cleared wave
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    An attrition arena, not a single boss: 4 spawns every 10 seconds over ~1 minute per wave, element rotating (fire → cold → acid → …) so players must re-gear resistances and swap tactics mid-fight. Reusable elemental blueprints exist; the timed trickle-wave controller is new script. These are trash adds, not registry bosses. Design: boss-updates#gimmick-swarm.

    Honesty guardrail: keep waves short and lethal so each element rotation forces a real counter-swap — the surround also pressures the healing economy.
  • Open two finished-but-unreachable endgame areas: Moria Deep Caverns below the Balrog, and the Temple of Nazgûl off Dol Guldur
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    Rounds out the new tier with two existing areas that only need a link-in and named bosses: Moria Deep Caverns (area020, descent from the Abyss of Moria — naturally gated by the Balrog) and the Temple of Nazgûl (area010, linked from Dol Guldur, already contains 7 encounters). Candidate bosses are all currently unspawned: Angmar the Evocator (capstone), Thuringwethil, Shadow Lord of Dol Guldur, Morgrond Captain, the Mutated Basilisks, Sewer Master. Design: boss-updates#tier-other.

    Second slice of Unlock Inacessible creatures; follows the Forbidden Realms Key tier. Tune each boss on placement per the tuning pass.
  • Remove boss crit immunities; rework Dev Crit to an extra multiplier or a stun instead
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: Piskan (Alec Cain)
  • The Wandering One: a roaming boss that adaptively builds damage-type immunity as it is hit, punishing mono-damage parties
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    Two ideas fused: it roams the module (no fixed wiki area — players must hunt it) and it learns to resist whatever hits it, drawing from a shared 500% immunity pool in 5–10% increments per damage type, stealing from the least-recently-received type when the pool is empty — so a party that rotates damage types keeps it off-balance. Design + feasibility: boss-updates#gimmick-borg.

    Build notes: reuses the unused wandering.nss state machine; must set NO_LEASH=1 or leash_to_area snaps it home; OnDamaged juggles percentage-immunity item properties across its gear slots; still qualifies as a single-instance registry boss (board attribution "roaming").

  • Gear to boost specific mage spells
    EnhancementUp nextRequested by: dc0960 (Dungeon_Crawler)
    Original ask: "An item to improve epic summons would be nice, not to make them more tanks, but more dps so they do something vs higher bosses. Or a way to improve spells in general, obviously there are a lot of spells that can't be used as they are — mobs are just immune to them. Like an item to change implosion to do 'chaos' dmg instead of death. As a whole for casters there are very little ways to kill something, its either damage shields, IGMS or epic spell lucky rolls for the majority. So some boosting to lvl 9 spells would be nice."

    The problem. Against high-tier bosses casters hit a wall: bosses are immune to death/instant-kill and to whole swaths of spells, so the only reliable answers left are damage shields, IGMS, and lucky epic-spell rolls. There is no way to make a favourite spell — or your epic summons — actually matter at the top of the roster.

    Concept: caster gear that drops from high-tier bosses. Each piece is a single equippable that flags its wearer on equip; a scripted hook then reads that flag plus the spell being cast and adjusts the outcome. Four archetypes:
    • Spell / School Amplifier — multiplies a chosen spell's or school's damage by a capped % (e.g. "Evocation +25%", or a single named spell). Rides the same spell-id effect-scaling machinery the module already uses to double spell durations.
    • Damage Retyper — rewrites a spell's damage type to an existing type the target isn't immune to (e.g. Implosion's death damage → magical or negative), so immune bosses still take the hit. No new damage type is being invented here — this maps only onto existing NWN types; the separate forge "true damage type" idea is out of scope for this item.
    • Summoner's Gear — pushes a summon's attack/damage output (DPS, not tankiness — exactly as asked) up to where it matters against high-CR bosses, extending the existing summon-scaling that already boosts summons at spawn.
    • Focus / Caster-Level Gear — temporarily bumps caster level or save DC for a chosen school so save-based level-9 spells can actually land on high-CR targets. The trickiest to balance — treat as a stretch tier.
    Feasibility. No native item property can boost a specific spell, so all four are scripted — but the plumbing already exists in the module: tag-based equip markers, the spell-cast hook (the same one that runs the Meaningwave counterspell), the spell-id effect-scaling handler that doubles durations, and the summon-boost hook. Reuse, not new engine work.

    Open questions. One dedicated slot with hard % caps and no stacking of the same effect; which specific spells/schools ship first; and the drop sources / rarity across the boss roster.

    Starter catalogue (2026-07-09). A concrete first draft of 20 items — five each for Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer and Wizard is written up, with signature effects, damage dice / caster-level scaling, stat lines tiered to each source boss's challenge rating, and a thematic boss drop for every one (all existing, reachable bosses — no new areas needed). A new design pillar rewards pure single-class casters with a stronger version of every effect.   • Weapons with both Attack Bonus (AB) and Enhancement Bonus (EB) appear to mis-calculate stats
    DefectShipped · Merit awardedJune 23rd, 2026Credit: Piskan (Alec Cain)

    Root cause is hardcoded NWN engine behavior, not a module bug: the Attack Bonus property and the attack component of the Enhancement Bonus property do not stack — the engine applies only the higher of the two to the attack roll — while the enhancement's damage and damage-reduction bypass always apply. A weapon carrying both therefore shows a confusing character sheet, and any Attack Bonus ≤ the Enhancement Bonus is dead weight.

    There is no global toggle for this, so we audited every weapon. 199 weapons (blueprints plus all placed/inventory instances) carried both a generic Attack Bonus and Enhancement Bonus; each was collapsed to a single Enhancement Bonus. Where Attack Bonus exceeded Enhancement, the kept value was set per-item to a balanced figure between the two. Driven by bin/ab-enhance-audit.py → ab-enhance-audit.csv → bin/ab-enhance-apply.py.

    Follow-up (player report: Chosen Kama still +13/+13): the first pass matched the Attack/Enhancement properties only when their unused subtype was encoded as 0, but NWN also encodes it as 65535 (0xFFFF). That hid 54 more weapons (Chosen Kama, the Tournament set, Spear of Destiny, Super Kama, Mindmelter, and others) from the audit, the blueprint collapse, and the player-inventory repair. All three tools now treat subtype 0 or 65535 as generic; the 54 were collapsed and the server vault was re-swept, fixing the carried copies in players' character files.


    NOTE:  Any old versions of items left in persistent storage boxes (Bank of Bree) must be pulled out of the storage and left in a character's inventory overnight to be fixed -- the daily 3am reboots will scan character .bic files adjust remove the AB bonus and fix (raise) the enchant bonus property to match items as documented in the wiki/aligned with their updated blueprints.
