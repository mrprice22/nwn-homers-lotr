Quest Guide — Homer's LOTR

This guide covers every quest and journal category in the module: what triggers them, how they progress, what they reward, and which ones are currently broken. A Working badge means the quest is fully scripted; Minor Bug means it functions but has a tag mismatch or minor issue; Broken means it cannot be completed; AWOL means it once existed or was planned but has no placed content.

Update (2026-05-28): The fixes described in this guide have been implemented in the module source. The status badges below reflect the post-fix state. Three earlier diagnoses in this guide were corrected during implementation: sc_001.nss never blocked Gloison's conversation (its Random(100) >= 100 branch can never fire, so it always returns TRUE); hgll_cliententer.nss had no pre-existing first-login check to hang the server-info journals from; and the Book of the Cora already carries useful properties. Changes still require an in-game pass — see UAT.

Update (2026-07-11) — audit pass: A re-audit against the live module found this guide had drifted. Corrections applied below: two fully-scripted reward quests were undocumented and are now listed (Paths of the Dead, Glorfindel's Curative); the Guilds server-info journal is not delivered (deliberately — the guild system is retired; see Server Info); and The Well of Souls was found unstartable — no Gondor Scribe NPC was placed. (That gap has since been fixed on 2026-07-13: the scribe is now placed and the quest is Working — see the card.) The six mod_* server-info journals that are delivered are now documented too.

Active Quests

Quests that can be started and completed by players today.

Bill Ferny's Ring Working

Journal tag: Ferny's Ring Giver: Bill Ferny, Bree Level range: 1–10 Reward: 500 XP + 200 gp (or 500 XP + keep Sharkey's Orders)

Bill Ferny, a shifty resident of Bree, claims an underling ran into a nearby cave with his ring. He asks you to retrieve it. A straightforward early quest that uncovers a darker conspiracy.

How to start: Speak to Bill Ferny in Bree.

Steps:
  1. Accept Ferny's request — journal entry 1 is set.
  2. Enter Bree Cave and recover the Ring of Sharkey from the bandits (or a container).
  3. Return to Ferny. The ring's inscription reads "Servant of Sharkey" — suspicious.
  4. At Ferny's house you will face an ambush. Two choices after:
    • Give Ferny back his letter (Sharkey's Orders) — 150 XP + 200 gp. Journal entry 4.
    • Keep the letter — 150 XP, no gold, but you retain an evidence item. Journal entry 3.
Journal entries:
  1. You agreed to help Ferny recover his ring from a bandit who fled into a nearby cave.
  2. You delivered the ring. The inscription "Servant of Sharkey" gives you pause. (Note: this entry appears set via a separate branch — see DM note.)
  3. An ambush awaited at Ferny's house. You kept the letter about Sharkey.
  4. An ambush awaited at Ferny's house. You gave Ferny his letter back.
Journal entry 2 is defined in the journal but the DLG Quest field for it points to the correct tag. The ring item (RingofSharkey) and Sharkey's Orders (SharkeysOrders) must be in inventory when speaking to Ferny. The ambush trigger is in ferny_convo2.dlg.json. XP: 500 via endferny1.nss (give letter) or aquirering.nss (keep letter) — raised from 150.

Known issue (2026-07-11): a legacy ported-campaign plot category p000 (struct 0, driven by p000c001ondeath.nss and the p000q001-005 scripts) is still wired in parallel with this custom Ferny's Ring chain, so a player can end up with two journal tracks for the same plot. Tracked as roadmap idea p000-duplicate-ferny-journal.

Ferny's Return Working

Journal tag: ferny_return Giver: Guardian of Bree, Bree Level range: ~3 (one-off) Reward: 200 gp + 250 XP + Ferny's Filching Gloves (Pick Pocket +2)

A prequel to Bill Ferny's Ring. Bill Ferny has been seen skulking around his old house — but Ferny himself has been standing in the market all the while. Someone is wearing Ferny's name, and the Bree watch is stretched too thin to find out who.

How to start: Ask any Guardian of Bree (the town guards, conversation b_guard_convo1.dlg) if anything strange is going on in town.

Steps:
  1. Accept the guard's request — q_fret_start.nss sets entry 1 and spawns the impostor in Bill Ferny's house.
  2. Confront the "Bill Ferny" squatting there (fret_impostor, conversation fret_imp.dlg) and call his bluff. He is one of Sharkey's ruffians, and he offers a choice:
    • Take his bribe — 75 gp from Ferny's own stash, and he slips out of Bree (q_fret_bribe.nss); or
    • Refuse and fight — he turns hostile (q_fret_fight.nss; stage advances on his death via q_fret_death.nss).
  3. Report back to any Guardian of Bree for the payout (q_fret_end.nss) — the report line differs by outcome, but both complete the quest.
Prequel bonus: completing this quest permanently unlocks a grateful greeting from Bill Ferny and a half-again payout on the Ferny's Ring turn-in (+100 gp on either ending, via endferny1.nss / aquirering.nss). The flag persists across relogs and reboots (campaign DB fret).

Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap ferny-return) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Admin action required: the impostor spawns at waypoint AP_ferny_return_1, which must be placed in billfernyshouse in the toolset (see admin-action-required.md). Until then the quest can be accepted but the house stands empty — the guard's stage-1 reminder line re-runs the spawn check on every conversation, so no player state is ever stranded. Quest state lives per character in the fret campaign DB (stages 0–3 + outcome), not in session-local ints.

  2. Travel south along the Greenway. You will find what happened to his son.
  3. Return and deliver the grim news to the Miller — journal entry 2. 450 XP awarded via at_003.nss (raised from 150).

  2. You delivered the sad news. The Miller grieves; his wife and his ale are small comfort.

The Miller's Other Son Working

Journal tag: bree_miller_son2 Giver: Han the Bree Miller, Bree Level range: ~8 (one-off) Reward: 500 gp + 500 XP + Magic Bag (good endings) / 200 XP (sad truth)

A sequel to The Miller's Son — offered only after that quest is complete. Han's other son, Dob, took his brother's death hard and fell in with a "trading crowd" on the road to Tharbad. The traders turned out to be cultists of the Red Eye, and their leader — a man they call the Voice — keeps the boy close.

How to start: Speak to Han the Bree Miller in Bree after completing The Miller's Son.

Steps:
  1. Accept Han's plea — q_mos2_start.nss sets entry 1 and spawns the quest NPCs.
  2. (Optional) Talk to Tolly the Peddler at Tharbad Bridge (mos2_ped.dlg) — a coin (10 gp) or a kind word buys directions to the cult camp (journal entry 2).
  3. Confront the Voice of the Red Eye in Tharbad (East) (mos2_cult.dlg). Three resolutions:
    • Persuade (DC 18: d20 + Persuade rank, one attempt per character, ever — q_mos2_persu.nss): he releases Dob peacefully. On a failed roll he attacks;
    • Fight — he turns hostile (q_mos2_fight.nss); his death frees the boy (q_mos2_death.nss, advances all party members in the area);
    • Walk away — leave Dob to the cult (q_mos2_leave.nss). You can still change your mind and fight him later.
  4. Report back to Han — outcome-specific lines. Freed endings pay 500 gp + 500 XP + a Magic Bag ("Dob's trading pack", q_mos2_end1.nss); the sad truth pays 200 XP (q_mos2_end2.nss).
Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap miller-other-son) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Admin action required: the two quest NPCs spawn at waypoints AP_millerotherson_1 (peddler, tharbadbridge) and AP_millerotherson_2 (cult leader, thardbadeast), which must be placed in the toolset (see admin-action-required.md). Until then the quest can be accepted but the road stands empty — Han's reminder line and the peddler's greeting re-run the spawn check, so no player state is ever stranded. Quest state lives per character in the mos2 campaign DB (stages 0–3 + outcome + the spent Persuade attempt), not in session-local ints. Gating on the original quest uses the session-local millerson == 2 or the persistent mos2/m1done flag now stamped by at_003.nss; characters who finished The Miller's Son before this shipped can simply re-run the original (its state is session-local) to qualify.

The Last Drop at Frogmorton Inn Working

Journal tag: frogmorton_ale Giver: Rigrin, the Whitfurrow Inn Level range: 1+ (daily, pure social) Reward: 25 gp + Frogmorton Pint (heals 4 hp, brief tipsy wobble)

The last cask of Frogmorton's famous harvest ale — the Floating Log's own autumn brew, carted over from Frogmorton before the brewer died — sits in Rigrin's cellar, and five patrons each claim the final drop. Rigrin judges the matter by the custom of the cask, an old rule that turns with the month, and he'll pay a neutral party to hear the claimants out and name the rightful drinker. No combat; pure Shire-tone dialogue.

How to start: Ask Rigrin at the Whitfurrow Inn (one area over from Frogmorton) what the common-room row is about.

Steps:
  1. Rigrin states this month's custom of the cask (e.g. "the coin that bought the barrel drains it") — twelve rules cycling through the year (q_frog_rulec.nss, keyed on GetCalendarMonth()).
  2. Chat with the five claimants in the common room — Marigold Bunce (brewed it), Poppy Thistlewool (paid for it), Hob Broadbelt (tapped it and drank first), Ned Puddifoot (hauled it from Frogmorton), Daisy Goodbody (won it at darts) — and work out who the rule points at.
  3. Give Rigrin your answer. One judgment per day: a right call pays 25 gp plus a Frogmorton Pint; a wrong call gets you gently laughed out of the room — either way the matter is settled until tomorrow.

The Frogmorton Pint is a single-use drink: 4 hp back and a short, purely cosmetic drunken stagger (q_frog_pint.nss). It cannot hurt you or anyone else.

Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap frogmorton-last-drop) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. This is the first consumer of the shared quest-cooldown library (quest_cd_inc.nss / the questcddb campaign DB): availability is gated by QCD_IsDoneToday (resets at UTC midnight, survives relogs and reboots) and Rigrin's come-back-tomorrow line shows the real time remaining via QCD_FmtSpan — so this quest doubles as UAT for the library. Venue note: the design doc placed five hobbits in frogmorton001, but that area has no NPCs and no inn interior; the quest instead reuses five existing commoner instances at the adjacent Whitfurrow Inn (renamed in place, new conversations q_frog_p1–p5) with the cask lore keeping its Frogmorton origin. Zero new placements.

Hobbit Post Working

Journal tag: hobbit_post Giver: Posco Whitfoot, post-master, Hobbiton Level range: all levels (daily, no combat) Reward: 60 gp base + 40 gp speed bonus + 50 XP; misdelivered parcels pay 60 gp extra and a small loot item

Posco Whitfoot has founded the Hobbit Post and needs an honest pair of legs: one sealed parcel per real day, delivered somewhere in the Shire for pay. The addressee rotates with the calendar day across five recipients — Daddy Twofoot (Hobbiton), Old Noakes (Bywater), the Keeper of the Green Dragon (Green Dragon Inn, door off Hobbiton), and Salvia Brockhouse and Milo Burrows (both in the Whitfurrow Inn common room). Pure low-pressure Shire flavour; no combat.

How to start: Talk to Posco Whitfoot in Hobbiton and take today's parcel.

Steps:
  1. Posco reads you the address off the label (it rotates daily, GetCalendarDay() % 5). Forgot where it's bound? Ask him again — he'll re-read the label, or write a fresh one.
  2. Carry the parcel to the named recipient and talk to them. The Shire judges its post by the freshness of the seal: land it within 5 real minutes of accepting and the fee rises from 60 gp to 100 gp.
  3. Rarely (8%) Posco's label is wrong: the named recipient refuses the parcel and points you at its true owner (another of the five), who pays 60 gp extra plus a small bonus item for untangling the post.
  4. Payment on delivery closes the day; the post-bag reopens at UTC midnight (Posco quotes the exact time remaining).

The parcel itself is plot-flagged and cursed — it can't be sold, dropped or traded, and re-accepting from Posco replaces any parcel you're already carrying, so they can never pile up.

Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap hobbit-post) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Second consumer of the shared quest-cooldown library (quest_cd_inc.nss / questcddb): QCD_IsDoneToday gates the offer and QCD_Stamp fires on payout; the speed bonus compares real-world epochs from QCD_Now() stored on the parcel (POST_T0), not the game clock. All six NPCs are existing placed instances — the postmaster and four recipients were renamed in place and split into dedicated blueprints (hobbit001_2/_3, nw_oldman_2_2, comfemale002_5, commale002_4) via bin/split-divergent-creatures.py so respawns keep their identities; the Green Dragon Inn keeper kept their name and simply gained gated parcel branches in greendragon1.dlg. Zero new placements, no waypoints needed.

Spider Silk Harvest Working

Journal tag: mirkwood_silk Giver: Thranduil, King of Eryn Lasgalen, Thranduil's Hall (through the Wood of Legolas, off Mirkwood: North) Level range: 12+ (daily kill-collect) Reward: 300 gp + 600 XP per day

The great spiders choke Thranduil's wood, but their silk is stronger than steel wire and his weavers prize it. Once per real day the Elvenking pays a flat bounty for six bolts of spider silk cut from the spiders of Mirkwood. The silk comes from the wood's named horrors — Giant Spider, Spider-Queen, Lava Climber, Death Weeper, Black Spear Spider and Sword Spider — which fill the encounters of every outdoor Mirkwood area (West, North, East, Central, Central East and Central South). The common base-game spiders and ettercaps mixed into the same encounters spin nothing worth carding.

How to start: Speak to Thranduil on his throne in Thranduil's Hall and accept the day's harvest (he turns away hunters below level 12).

Steps:
  1. Accept the harvest from Thranduil — the journal opens and the day's hunt goes live.
  2. Kill the named spiders anywhere beneath Mirkwood's boughs. Each qualifying kill has a 75% chance to cut a Bolt of Spider Silk straight into your pack (kills by your summons and companions credit you, same rule as the bestiary). Drops stop at six bolts carried — silk cannot be hoarded past a day's need.
  3. Return to Thranduil with six bolts and turn them in: 300 gp + 600 XP, bolts consumed, day closed.
  4. The bounty reopens at UTC midnight; until then Thranduil quotes the exact time remaining.

Bolts drop only while your own hunt is active (accepted today, not yet paid), so a party member without the quest gets nothing and there is no stockpiling across days. Spare bolts are worth a few coppers as vendor trash at most.

Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap spider-silk-harvest) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Third consumer of the shared quest-cooldown library (quest_cd_inc.nss / questcddb): the active flag is itself a calendar stamp (mirkwood_silk_acc), so the hunt state survives relogs and restarts with no state item. Silk drops chain through the blueprint OnDeath slot (q_silk_d1/d2/d3 on the six module spider blueprints), which the bestiary wrapper stores and chains at spawn, and each wrapper re-chains the family's original reward script (gpondeath / 350ondeathtopart / sb_creaturekill) — kill counts, gold and XP all still fire. The giver is the existing placed Thranduil (thranduil_2, also a Roll-of-the-Fallen boss — the new q_silk conversation is set identically on blueprint and instance, so respawns keep it). Zero new placements, no waypoints needed. The Quest Ideas dynamic-pricing variant (server-wide silk_supply moving the price) is deferred to a possible v2; v1 pays a flat bounty.

The Riddle Game Working

Journal tag: riddle_game Giver: the Whispering Wretch, Bree Cave (down the cave mouth off Bree) Level range: 1+ (weekly dialogue minigame — no combat) Reward on a win: 100–300 gp + 250 XP + one random trinket from the hoard

Something pale and whispering squats at the water's edge in the cave beneath Bree-hill, and it wants to play the old game: seven riddles, asked in the classic Hobbit style and answered by picking one of four answers. Guess five or more of the seven rightly and it grudgingly surrenders a present from its little hoard; guess fewer and you get nothing but wet chuckling. It plays once per real week per character, win or lose — the riddles "must ripen".

How to start: Enter Bree Cave and speak to the Whispering Wretch; accept the challenge. Open to all levels.

Steps:
  1. Accept the game — the journal opens and the wretch asks riddle one of seven.
  2. Answer each riddle from the four choices. The wretch heckles you between riddles either way; the running score is kept for you.
  3. After the seventh answer the game resolves on the spot: 5+ right pays 100–300 gp, 250 XP and one random minor treasure (a lesser ring, a gem or a potent potion); fewer pays nothing.
  4. The game reopens when the calendar week turns (Monday 00:00 UTC); until then the wretch quotes the exact time remaining.

The riddle set changes weekly — everyone on the server gets the same seven riddles in a given week, drawn from a larger bank, and the answer order shuffles per riddle. Walking away (or relogging) mid-game forfeits nothing: the week is only spent when the seventh answer lands, and the wretch will restart the same week's game from riddle one.

Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap riddle-game) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Fourth consumer of the shared quest-cooldown library (quest_cd_inc.nss / questcddb), calendar-weekly via QCD_IsDoneThisWeek. The wretch (q_rid_wretch) is a plot, non-hostile clone of the Gollum blueprint — strongly implied to be, never named — script-spawned at waypoint AP_riddlegame_1 from the Bree Cave OnEnter wrapper (q_rid_enter); the waypoint must be placed in the toolset before the wretch appears (scripts no-op gracefully until then; see admin-action-required.md). The placed CR-1029 Gollum boss and its Roll-of-the-Fallen tracking are untouched. One generic riddle dialogue node serves the whole bank via custom tokens 6360–6368; the 16-riddle bank, weekly seeded selection (%Y%W stride walk) and reward table live in q_rid_inc.nss.

Beorn's Garden Working

Journal tag: beorn_garden Giver: Grimbeorn the Old, Lord of the Beornings, at Beorn's homestead (off the Old Forest Road) Level range: 12+ (daily) Reward: 250–450 gp + 700 XP + Beorn's Honeycomb (sometimes two, or a Cure Serious draught besides)

A warg pack has been raiding the bee-meadows below the Carrock, and Grimbeorn has hives to mend and no hands to spare. The day's work has two halves that feed each other: harvest the three great honey hives (hollow stumps — one in Beorn's garden, one on each Carrock height) and skin six warg pelts from the raiders. There are no wargs standing about waiting to be farmed — cracking a hive open is what calls the pack in: each harvest spawns two Garden-Raiding Wargs (CR 6) right at the stump.

How to start: Speak to Grimbeorn the Old at the homestead; level 12+ required, once per real day per character.

Steps:
  1. Accept the garden work — the journal opens and the hives become harvestable for you.
  2. Use each of the three Honey Hives once (per-hive, per-day — floaty text counts "N of 3"). Each harvest spawns a warg pack at the hive.
  3. Kill wargs while the quest is active; the killing blow's party owner skins a pelt per warg ("N of 6"). Short on pelts with all hives drawn? Rattle any emptied hive to call another pack — the day can always be finished (spawns are capped per area so hives can't be clicked into a flood).
  4. Return to Grimbeorn with 6 pelts and 3 combs drawn: he takes the pelts and pays 250–450 gp, 700 XP and honeycomb from Beorn's stores (d100: always one comb; 61+ a second comb; 86+ a Cure Serious Wounds potion instead of the second comb).
  5. The day resets at UTC midnight; until then Grimbeorn quotes the exact time remaining.
Added 2026-07-14 (roadmap beorns-garden) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Fifth consumer of the shared quest-cooldown library (quest_cd_inc.nss / questcddb), calendar-daily via QCD_IsDoneToday; the active flag and the per-hive harvest locks are item-free calendar stamps (beorn_garden_acc, beorn_garden_h1..3), so state survives relogs and restarts. The hives (q_brn_hive, tag HoneyHive, CEP stump) are script-spawned by q_brn_spawn from the beorn/carrok/carrokgreater OnEnter wrappers — the three waypoints AP_beornsgarden_1/2/3 must be placed in the toolset before any hive appears (scripts no-op gracefully until then; see admin-action-required.md). Wargs (q_brn_warg) are quest-spawned only — no standing encounters were touched — and their OnDeath wrapper q_brn_wd chains nw_c2_default7, staying bestiary-safe under bst_install. Grimbeorn's new conversation (q_brn_conv) was set on blueprint and placed instance both; progress tokens 6370–6372.

The Twentieth Plot of Mazarbul Working

Journal tag: mazarbul_20 Giver: Frár the Restless, a shade of Balin's colony, in the Chamber of Records (Moria) Level range: 18+ (one-off) Reward: 1200 gp + 2000 XP + Seal of the Twentieth (Damage Reduction +1/Soak 5, Damage Resistance: Negative 10/-)

A hidden codicil to the Book of Mazarbul names twenty burial plots whose seal-fires keep the dead of Moria at rest. Nineteen were lit before the colony fell — the twentieth never was, and what lies beneath it does not lie still. Frár, a restless dwarf-shade among the toppled record-stones, asks you to finish his colony's last labor: light the three crypt-seal braziers (one in the Chamber of Records, two in Balin's Tomb), then destroy the wraith that rises to smother the third flame.

How to start: Speak to Frár the Restless in the Chamber of Records; level 18+ required, once per character (one-off — the seal, once set, holds).

Steps:
  1. Accept Frár's charge — the journal opens and the seal-braziers will take your flame.
  2. Use each of the three Crypt-Seal Braziers once (per-character, persistent — floaty text counts "N of 3"; each lighting also grants a short ward against grave-chill, Negative resist 5/- for 5 minutes).
  3. The third brazier raises the Wraith of the Twentieth Plot (CR 21). Destroy it — the killing blow's party credits every party member in the area who is at the wraith stage. If the wraith is slain while you are absent (or you fall and return), stir any brazier's flame to re-summon it, so the fight can always be re-staged.
  4. Return to Frár: 1200 gp, 2000 XP and the Seal of the Twentieth. The quest closes for good.
Added 2026-07-15 (roadmap twentieth-plot-mazarbul) — scripted and built, pending in-game UAT. Per-character persistent state in the maz20 campaign DB (stage + seal_1..3), same one-off scheme as Ferny's Return, so progress survives relogs and restarts and the reward can never be re-earned. Everything is script-spawned: Frár (q_maz_ghost, a Plot dwarf commoner with a permanent ghostly visage) and the braziers (q_maz_braz, CEP dungeon brazier, OnUsed q_maz_use) come from the chamberofrecords/balinstomb OnEnter wrappers (q_maz_ent1/2, chaining the areas' previous scripts) — the waypoints AP_mazarbul20_1 (ghost) and AP_mazarbul20_2/3/4 (braziers) must be placed in the toolset before anything appears (scripts no-op gracefully until then; optional AP_mazarbul20_5 sets a wraith arena spot, otherwise it rises at the third-lit brazier — see admin-action-required.md). The wraith (q_maz_wraith) is a detuned clone of the Witch King's wraith (CR 21 / 420 hp), single-spawn guarded, OnDeath wrapper q_maz_wd chains nw_c2_default7 (bestiary-safe). Elrond and his "Elrond's Request" thread were deliberately left untouched — the ghost is the sole quest giver. Progress token 6380.

Elrond's Request Working

Journal tag: Elrond's Request Giver: Elrond, Rivendell Level range: 15–30 Reward: 2,000 XP + Elrond's Writ

Elrond of Rivendell charges you with two tasks: break the Uruk-hai siege of the Troll Halls and retrieve the Helm of Azog the Warlord, then venture into Moria and find evidence of Balin's Expedition (the Dwarf Head). This is the module's main mid-game quest chain.

How to start: Speak to Elrond in Rivendell and accept both tasks. Journal entry 1 is set via the DLG engine's built-in Quest field.

Steps:
  1. Kill Azog the Warlord (CR 14) in the Angmar Mountain area and loot the Helm of the Warlord.
  2. Push into Balin's Tomb in Moria and retrieve the Dwarf Head (evidence of Balin's expedition).
  3. Return to Elrond with both items in your party's inventory. Script sc_015 checks for both.
  4. Agree to accept your reward — at_026 destroys both items; at_024 gives 2,000 XP and the Elrond's Writ item. Journal entry 2 set.
Journal entries:
  1. Elrond has given you two tasks: break the Uruk-hai siege of the Trollshaws and bring the Helm of Azog; venture into Moria and find sign of Balin's Expedition.
  2. Both tasks are complete. You now have a new place to call home.

Fixed (2026-05-28): The reply node in elrondconv.dlg.json that set the bogus tag Category000 (QuestEntry 1) now sets Elrond's Request. General balancing principle: NWN1 EE characters gain roughly 1,000–3,000 XP per level in the early tiers from combat alone. A quest reward should represent a meaningful bump — roughly half a level's worth of combat XP at the quest's target tier. Early quests (levels 1–10) should give 400–800 XP; mid quests (15–30) should give 1,500–3,000 XP; end-game quests 3,000–8,000 XP.

Item rewards: Most quests give XP and gold only. Adding a unique named item to each quest — even a minor one — significantly increases perceived value and gives players something to talk about. Items don't need to be powerful; they need to be memorable.

Investigating Player Reports

Three historical bug reports, traced to their roots.

"The Kallrist Island tiger quest is AWOL"

Verdict: Quest existed but had no journal tracking — now resolved.

The Kallrist Tiger Hunt was always fully functional — NPC Sald, the tiger creatures, the Kallrist Tiger Heart item, and the 200 gp payout all work. Players never saw it in their quest log because there was no journal category. Fixed: a Kallrist Tiger Hunt category was added and wired into Sald's accept node (at_sald01.nss), so the hunt now shows in the journal.

"The demon north of Bree quest is broken — gave 1,000 XP, chicken feed by the time you can kill it"

Verdict: No demon exists near Bree today. The 1,000 XP report points to the Well of Souls.

A thorough search of all creature placements in and around Bree and the surrounding north areas finds no demon creature. The 1,000 XP reward described matches at_008.nss — the Gondor Scribe's payout for bringing Azagoth's Head (Well of Souls quest completion). Azagoth spawns in the Ruins of Annuminas north of Bree (via an encounter), not in Moria. Either:

  • A demon NPC was placed near Bree by a DM in the past and later removed from the GIT, or
  • The player was describing the Azagoth / Well of Souls payout.

Either way, the 1,000 XP complaint was valid — the at_008.nss payout was raised to 4,000 XP (and is slated for 10,000). Note, however, that the quest is currently unstartable because no Gondor Scribe is placed — see The Well of Souls.

"Elrond's quest is fixed, but existing journals (server updates) got removed when accepting it"

Verdict: Elrond's scripts never clear any journal entries. The server-info journals were simply never given.

No script in the module calls RemoveJournalQuestEntry or any equivalent function. The Elrond quest sets entries via the DLG engine's built-in Quest field, which only adds entries and cannot remove existing ones.

The real problem: mod_enter.nss (which contained the server rules/guilds/website journal delivery) was dead code, not wired to any event, so players never received those entries. Before and after speaking to Elrond, the server-info journals were absent — because they were never given. The timing coincidence made it look like Elrond's quest was clearing them.

Partly fixed (corrected 2026-07-11): AddJournalQuestEntry calls for rules, website and the six mod_* journals were added directly to hgll_cliententer.nss, the real OnClientEnter handler, and run idempotently on every login so all characters receive them in their Completed section. The guilds journal is not included — deliberately, as of 2026-07-15: the guild system is retired/suspended, so its journal is withheld on purpose. See the Server Info Journals card. The forum URL in the website entry has since been refreshed to the community Discord.
