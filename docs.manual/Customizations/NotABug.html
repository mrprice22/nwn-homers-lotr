Looks Like a Bug — Isn't

A handful of things on this server look broken the first time you meet them and are working exactly as intended. If one of these just happened to you, you are not the first — follow the link for the full explanation.

A feat is listed twice on my character sheet, and I only picked it once
Expected for casters at class level 40 and above. One of the two is an inert stand-in that exists so the level-up screen stops offering you a feat you already own. You get the benefit once, and it can appear on its own without you picking anything. See Why a feat can show twice.
Level-up offers me no spells to choose past 40 — every tab is empty
That screen belongs to the game client and cannot be repaired from the server. Do not worry about it — just finish the level-up and wait a few seconds. A Spellbook Study window opens by itself and hands you every pick the screen ate. Close it and your picks are still banked; finish a rest and it opens again. See Spellbook Study.
My sorcerer or bard cannot swap known spells past 40
A known limitation of the same client screen, not something you have done wrong. You keep every spell you know and gain more casts per day of all of them. Tracked on the Roadmap — see Spellbook Study.
The boss healed itself and got stronger when I backed off
Deliberate, and only the unique bosses do it. Disengaging from one of them is meant to cost you. See Boss Enrage on Retreat.
Devastating Critical stopped killing things outright
The stock save-or-die was removed on purpose, for you and for everything you fight. It now lands as a very large hit instead. See Critical Hits & Devastating Critical.
I was healed and then could not be healed again for a while
Full-restore healing is a timed resource here rather than a button you can hold. See Soul-fatigue on Heal.
My shifted form shows the wrong attack and damage on the character sheet
The merge worked — the sheet just doesn't redraw for it. When you polymorph, your gear's magical properties are copied onto the form's natural attacks and hide, but the character sheet keeps showing the shape's own base numbers, so your attack bonus and damage look far lower than they are. Trust the combat log instead: roll a few attacks and you will see the real to-hit and the full damage breakdown, bonuses included. Nothing is lost and nothing needs re-equipping. See Shapeshifting: Full Equipment Merge.
I was turned to stone and the death window won't let me Respawn
Petrification here is a timed death, not an instant one. For the first couple of minutes you are a statue and the game's own death window shows Respawn greyed out — that is deliberate breathing room, and it is there for a reason: shout for help. Anyone nearby can still cure you with Stone to Flesh or a Restoration-family cure, and if they reach you in time the countdown is cancelled outright and you walk away alive. If nobody does, the stone comes off, you die properly, and the death window returns with Respawn enabled — from that point you can respawn at your bind point or be raised or resurrected like any other death. Chat and floating text count the time down for you the whole way.
An item I picked up vanished into a roll window
Nobody stole it. Valuable drops go to a Need / Greed / Pass roll when party members are nearby. See Party Loot Rolls.
