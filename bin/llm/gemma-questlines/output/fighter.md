
## Quest 1: The Toll of Iron   (Level 1 • hunt & tracking • Apprentice)
**Hook:** A desperate local magistrate hires you to track a band of deserters who stole a shipment of grain and, more importantly, a cache of siege-supplies intended for the front lines. Only a seasoned warrior who can read the "language of the mud"—the signs of a retreating army—can find them before they vanish into the deeper wilds.
**Setting:** The Gray Weald, a sodden, mist-choked forest of skeletal oaks where the boundary between the civilized road and the hungry wild blurs.
**Objectives:** 1) Track the deserters' broken trail through the Weald. 2) Ambush the group to recover the supplies. 3) Decide whether to execute the deserters or take them in as prisoners to turn over to the magistrate.
**Key NPCs / Foes:** 
- *Captain Vane:* A weary, pragmatic officer who views the deserters as a stain on his record.
- *Kaelen the Hollow:* The deserter leader, a broken man seeking a life of quiet theft over a life of loud death.
**Twist / Choice:** You discover the deserters aren't just stealing for profit; they are fleeing a "Shadow-blight" in the southern camps. Forgiving them keeps your conscience clean but risks a local uprising; turning them in ensures the magistrate's favor but marks you as a man of cold iron.
**Class Flavor:** Emphasizes the Fighter's role as a tracker of martial movements, utilizing Athletics for navigation and intimidation/strength for the confrontation.
**Rewards:**
- *Item:* Soldier's Grit Blade — *Longsword*; +1 to hit/damage; requires 1 hour of "sharpening" (meditation) daily to maintain its edge; a blade notched by a hundred skirmishes.
- *Class Perk:* Tactical Awareness — You gain a bonus to Initiative when fighting in terrain that offers cover.
- *Also:* 50 Gold, 100 XP, Reputation: "Reliable."
**Balance Note:** The +1 bonus is minor at level 1, and the daily maintenance requirement prevents the blade from being a "set and forget" power.
SYNOPSIS: You successfully recovered the siege supplies from the deserters in the Gray Weald, establishing your reputation as a competent tracker of broken men.
