
## Quest 1: The Ledger of Silent Sins   (Level 1 • Investigation/Mystery • Apprentice)
**Hook:** A "Shadow-Broker" of the guild—an aging, blind informant—claims your initiation begins not with a kill, but with a discovery. They need a shadow who can move through the courtly circles of a dying noble house to find a specific name in a private registry.
**Setting:** The Velascor Estate, a crumbling manor of rotting velvet and weeping stone, where the air tastes of dust and old secrets.
**Objectives:** 1) Infiltrate the estate's archives during a funeral wake. 2) Identify the true owner of a "Black Ledger" hidden within the family vault. 3) Extract the ledger without alerting the estate's hired "Watchmen."
**Key NPCs / Foes:** Lord Velascor (a decaying patriarch seeking to preserve his name); The Grave-Watch (low-level mercenaries tasked with guarding the vault).
**Twist / Choice:** You discover the ledger contains names of commoners—not just lords—who were "liquidated" for debt. Do you report the full list to the Guild (securing your standing) or redact the commoners' names to preserve the Guild's "noble" reputation?
**Class Flavor:** Emphasizes stealth mechanics, perception checks for detecting guards, and the "moral weight" of the Assassin's target selection.
**Rewards:**
- *Item:* Whispering Needle — Dagger; +1 to hit and grants a bonus to Stealth checks while held; requires "Blood-Bond" (must be used to kill a sentient being to remain sharp); a needle-thin blade of blackened steel.
- *Class Perk:* Ghost-Step — Once per long rest, you can move through a space occupied by a hostile creature without provoking an attack.
- *Also:* 150 Gold / Apprentice Reputation / Access to the "Grey Market" information hub.
**Balance Note:** The +1 and Stealth bonus are significant at level 1, but the Blood-Bond requirement ensures the player remains tethered to the class's grim identity.
SYNOPSIS: The Assassin successfully infiltrated the Velascor Estate and recovered a ledger of debts, making their first move into the Guild's web of secrets.
