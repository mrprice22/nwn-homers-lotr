
## Quest 1: The Marrow of the Frost-Wolf   (Level 1 • survival/wilderness • Apprentice)
**Hook:** A village elder, the last keeper of your clan's oral history, provides a map to a sacred burial cairn and begs you to retrieve a relic to "warm the blood" of the survivors.
**Setting:** The Whispering Tundra—a desolate, white-washed expanse where the wind screams like a dying woman and the permafrost hides the bones of the old world.
**Objectives:** 1) Navigate the blizzard to reach the cairn. 2) Defend against scavenging carrion-beasts. 3) Retrieve the "Frost-Heart" shard from the center of the site.
**Key NPCs / Foes:** Elder Hrolf (desperate to preserve his lineage); The Carrion-Hounds (starving, mutated wolves driven by a lingering hunger).
**Twist / Choice:** Upon reaching the cairn, you find the shard is being used to keep a small, shivering spirit-child from freezing; you must choose to take the shard (securing your clan's strength) or leave it (saving the spirit but remaining weak).
**Class Flavor:** Emphasizes the Barbarian's endurance against extreme environments and their raw physical prowess in close-quarters survival.
**Rewards:**
- *Item:* Wolf-Tooth Pendant — Worn; grants +1 to survival checks in extreme cold; requires "Blood-Bond" (must be bathed in the blood of a predator once per moon); a token of the clan's lost hunt.
- *Class Perk:* Northern Grit — Once per long rest, when taking cold damage, you can ignore the first 5 points of damage and gain a temporary burst of adrenaline (speed increase).
- *Also:* 50 Gold; Clan Reputation: Scavenger.
**Balance Note:** The "Blood-Bond" and damage threshold are low-level, ensuring the Barbarian feels hardy without breaking early-game action economy.
SYNOPSIS: You braved the Whispering Tundra and reclaimed a shard of the Frost-Heart, marking your first step toward reclaiming your clan's shattered legacy.

## Quest 2: The Ossuary of the Red-Silt   (Level 5 • Hunt & Tracking • Apprentice)
**Hook:** An elder of the scattered clans, dying of a "spirit-fever," identifies the specific scent of the marauding warlord’s vanguard—a pack of hounds bred to hunt your kin. Only a Barbarian’s primal senses can track the lingering blood-trail through the shifting scree.
**Setting:** The Bone-Choked Ravine, a jagged scar in the earth where the wind whistles through ribcages of long-dead giants and the soil is stained a permanent, rusted crimson.
**Objectives:** 1) Track the "Blood-Hounds" through the ravine's labyrinthine geography. 2) Identify and neutralize the pack's alpha. 3) Recover the "Tether-Stone," a clan relic stolen from the vanguard's loot.
**Key NPCs / Foes:** 
- *Vargos the Hound-Master:* A scarred man who views the Barbarian as a trophy to be broken.
- *The Iron-Tooth Pack:* Six spectral-infused hounds that explode in mist upon death.
**Twist / Choice:** The player finds a wounded scout from a rival clan. They can execute the scout to ensure no witnesses remain (gaining "Ruthless" reputation) or provide aid (losing time and risking the hounds' attention).
**Class Flavor:** Emphasizes the Barbarian's "Primal Awareness" and "Rage" mechanics as tools for tracking and survival against pack-based enemies.
**Rewards:**
- *Item:* Fang of the Ravine — Weapon (Greatsword/Greataxe); adds +1d6 necrotic damage; requires a "Blood Sacrifice" (the wielder must take 1d4 damage to activate); a jagged tooth from a spirit-hound.
- *Class Perk:* Scent of the Prey — While in a Rage, the Barbarian gains Advantage on Perception checks to track and can see the "heat" of living creatures through heavy fog or darkness.
- *Also:* 500 Gold; +10 Clan Reputation.
**Balance Note:** The damage bonus is high, but the self-inflicted damage ensures the player cannot spam the bonus without managing their HP pool.
SYNOPSIS: The Barbarian tracked and slew the Hound-Master's pack, recovering a stolen relic and proving their ability to hunt the warlord's shadow.
