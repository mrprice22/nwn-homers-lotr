
## Quest 1: The Weaver’s Toll   (Level 1 • Investigation/Mystery • Apprentice)
**Hook:** A Master of the Silver-String Order suspects a blight in the local grove; they require an initiate who can "see" the weave of magic to identify where the starlight is being bled dry.
**Setting:** The Whisper-Glass Woods, where the trees possess translucent leaves that chime like bells in the wind, now unnaturally silent.
**Objectives:** 1) Track the trail of "hollowed" flora where magic has been drained. 2) Identify the specific frequency of the shadow-leech's signature. 3) Secure a sample of the corrupted sap for the Order's archives.
**Key NPCs / Foes:** Elara Moonwhisper (The Mentor, seeking to preserve the grove); The Shimmer-Wight (A minor shadow-construct that mimics the sound of weeping leaves).
**Twist / Choice:** The Archer discovers the "leech" isn't an invading monster but a dying, corrupted dryad. They can choose to "purify" her (risking her death to stop the drain) or "seal" her (preserving her life but allowing the drain to continue at a slower rate).
**Class Flavor:** Focuses on the "Arcane" detection of magic and the "Archer" precision of tracking minute environmental changes.
**Rewards:**
- *Item:* Quill-Tip Arrowhead — Ammunition; +1 to hit against Shadow-touched creatures; requires a "focusing" gesture (takes 1 round to prep); forged from the feathers of a Void-Hawk.
- *Class Perk:* Sight of the Weave — Passive; allows the Archer to detect active magical effects within 30ft, but causes minor exhaustion if used more than 3 times per day.
- *Also:* 50 Gold; 100 XP; "Initiate" status with the Silver-String Order.
**Balance Note:** The +1 bonus is minor, but the "Shadow-touched" restriction ensures it scales specifically with the questline's primary antagonists.
SYNOPSIS: The Archer identified the first instance of starlight-drainage and discovered the tragic source of the corruption in the Whisper-Glass Woods.
