Bosses & Difficulty

  • Forbidden Realms Key tier: journal quest and a Númenor gate opening the orphaned Tombs of the Lost Souls as the Weathertop court's barrow
    EnhancementNeeds manual finishingRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    The Forbidden Realms Key has been stealable from Summanus for as long as anyone can remember, and until now it opened precisely nothing. It opens something now. Come by the key and a journal opens telling you what the seven marks cut into its ward actually are — grave-wards. The stone that answers it is a sealed slab in Númenor: Noirinan, and beyond it lie the Tombs of the Lost Souls: not a ruin but a court, whose lords have not gone quiet. The Weathertop King, his Queen and their Archer still hold their places. Bring a party. Full walkthrough: QuestGuide → The Forbidden Realms.
  • Unlock Inacessible creatures, beef up stats, give them areas to live in
    EnhancementNeeds design inputRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    Many creatures exist in the module but have never been placed anywhere in the world. A concrete plan to bring them in has been written and split into two pieces of work: opening the Forbidden Realms Key tier (the Tombs of the Lost Souls, housing the unspawned Weathertop court) and opening Moria Deep Caverns and the Temple of Nazgûl (Angmar, Thuringwethil, basilisks, the Sewer Master and more). The plan: Endgame Difficulty design brief.
  • A new low-dice 'true' damage type no boss is immune to — made incompatible with the forge so you choose optimized gear vs. boss-chipping gear
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: Tukwut
  • Elemental swarm arena: waves of 24 elementals trickle-spawned to surround the party, the element rotating each cleared wave
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    An elemental swarm arena: not one boss but waves of two dozen elementals trickling in to surround the party, with the element rotating each time a wave is cleared — fire, then cold, then acid, and on. You cannot pre-gear for it; you have to re-resist and change tactics mid-fight. Design: boss-updates#gimmick-swarm.
  • Open two finished-but-unreachable endgame areas: Moria Deep Caverns below the Balrog, and the Temple of Nazgûl off Dol Guldur
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    Rounds out the new tier with two existing areas that only need a link-in and named bosses: Moria Deep Caverns (area020, descent from the Abyss of Moria — naturally gated by the Balrog) and the Temple of Nazgûl (area010, linked from Dol Guldur, already contains 7 encounters). Candidate bosses are all currently unspawned: Angmar the Evocator (capstone), Thuringwethil, Shadow Lord of Dol Guldur, Morgrond Captain, the Mutated Basilisks, Sewer Master. Design: boss-updates#tier-other.

    Second slice of Unlock Inacessible creatures; follows the Forbidden Realms Key tier. Tune each boss on placement per the tuning pass.
  • Remove boss crit immunities; rework Dev Crit to an extra multiplier or a stun instead
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: Piskan (Alec Cain)
  • The Wandering One: a roaming boss that adaptively builds damage-type immunity as it is hit, punishing mono-damage parties
    EnhancementLaterRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    The Wandering One — a proposed boss with no fixed home: it roams the module, so players have to hunt it rather than look it up. It also learns, building resistance to whatever damage type you keep hitting it with and shedding resistance to types you have stopped using — so a party that rotates its damage keeps it off balance, and a mono-damage party grinds to a halt. Design and feasibility: boss-updates#gimmick-borg.
  • Clone/parent boss: a chance an existing boss fight is gate-crashed by the boss's 'Mom' — modest stat boost, better gear, two-front surprise
    EnhancementUnder considerationRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    A chance that a boss fight you thought you knew gets gate-crashed by the boss's mother — a modestly tougher version with better gear, arriving mid-fight and forcing a snap decision about which one to deal with first. A threat multiplier and a surprise, not a second damage sponge. Design: boss-updates#gimmick-parent.
  • Opt-in curse: volunteer for extra damage taken and doubled soul-fatigue in exchange for better rarity-tiered socketed-gem drops
    EnhancementUnder considerationRequested by: HomelessSon (Server Admin)
    Difficulty as a player choice instead of a wall: take the curse (≈50% more damage taken and/or doubled soul-fatigue) for a materially better reward roll. The reward vehicle rides on the gem-socketing system (Socket system for weapons) — the curse just bumps the gem rarity band / drop chance, no separate loot system. Design: boss-updates#curse.

    Depends on both the healing-economy levers and the socket/gem system shipping first; chance-based loot itself is greenfield for the module.

    This is working as intended: The Balrog currently has no heal potions or spells, should we add some?
  • Boss tuning so you can progress past level 40 (starter bosses are currently unkillable)

  • Gear to boost specific mage spells
    EnhancementUp nextRequested by: dc0960 (Dungeon_Crawler)
    Original ask: "An item to improve epic summons would be nice, not to make them more tanks, but more dps so they do something vs higher bosses. Or a way to improve spells in general, obviously there are a lot of spells that can't be used as they are — mobs are just immune to them. Like an item to change implosion to do 'chaos' dmg instead of death. As a whole for casters there are very little ways to kill something, its either damage shields, IGMS or epic spell lucky rolls for the majority. So some boosting to lvl 9 spells would be nice."

    The problem. Against high-tier bosses casters hit a wall: bosses are immune to death/instant-kill and to whole swaths of spells, so the only reliable answers left are damage shields, IGMS, and lucky epic-spell rolls. There is no way to make a favourite spell — or your epic summons — actually matter at the top of the roster.

    Concept: caster gear that drops from high-tier bosses. Each piece is a single equippable that flags its wearer on equip; a scripted hook then reads that flag plus the spell being cast and adjusts the outcome. Four archetypes:
    • Spell / School Amplifier — multiplies a chosen spell's or school's damage by a capped % (e.g. "Evocation +25%", or a single named spell). Rides the same spell-id effect-scaling machinery the module already uses to double spell durations.
    • Damage Retyper — rewrites a spell's damage type to an existing type the target isn't immune to (e.g. Implosion's death damage → magical or negative), so immune bosses still take the hit. No new damage type is being invented here — this maps only onto existing NWN types; the separate forge "true damage type" idea is out of scope for this item.
    • Summoner's Gear — pushes a summon's attack/damage output (DPS, not tankiness — exactly as asked) up to where it matters against high-CR bosses, extending the existing summon-scaling that already boosts summons at spawn.
    • Focus / Caster-Level Gear — temporarily bumps caster level or save DC for a chosen school so save-based level-9 spells can actually land on high-CR targets. The trickiest to balance — treat as a stretch tier.
    Feasibility. No native item property can boost a specific spell, so all four are scripted — but the plumbing already exists in the module: tag-based equip markers, the spell-cast hook (the same one that runs the Meaningwave counterspell), the spell-id effect-scaling handler that doubles durations, and the summon-boost hook. Reuse, not new engine work.

    Open questions. One dedicated slot with hard % caps and no stacking of the same effect; which specific spells/schools ship first; and the drop sources / rarity across the boss roster.

A concrete first draft of 20 items — five each for Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer and Wizard is written up, with signature effects, damage dice / caster-level scaling, stat lines tiered to each source boss's challenge rating, and a thematic boss drop for every one (all existing, reachable bosses — no new areas needed). A new design pillar rewards pure single-class casters with a stronger version of every effect.
