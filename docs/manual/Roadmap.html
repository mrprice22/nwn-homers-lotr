    DefectNeeds manual finishingRequested by: Szescian82

Reported by Szescian82, who was jailed for a Mithral of Sauron whose -30% he had removed at a forge months before the rule existed.

    The forges' "Minus 10%" option really gives minus 10% now. It was applying PLUS 10% arcane spell failure - a penalty - at every anvil, and since the drawback rule shipped no smith would take it back off. If you bought that option, speak to a Forge Warden or to the admin.

    The law now says what it objects to. Being jailed used to tell you only which item offended. It now names the bane that was struck from the piece, or the ceiling it breached ("7 enchantments where the law allows 6"), and the Forge Warden repeats the same detail. A true bane is still unliftable - that part has not changed.

  • Encounter Spawns start breaking from level 41-60 (not spawning strong enough mobs)
    DefectNeeds manual finishingRequested by: -Methonash-
    once you're level 60, some encounters isn't working properly, like not giving the right enemies. In Dimholt for example the Thing of the Dark doesn't spawn with it's encounter. In Mirkwood Central South, the high level spiders doesn't spawn, instead you get only Giant Spiders and a Spider-Queen, CR 21 and CR 27. Most definitely happens after level 41+, I imagine.

    Fixed 2026-08-24. The encounters were reading stale numbers about their own creatures. Every encounter keeps its own note of how tough each creature in its pool is, written down once when the creature was added and never updated since. The creatures were rebalanced for levels 41-60; those notes were not.

    The game fills an encounter by working down from the toughest creature it thinks is in the pool until it has spent the difficulty budget for your level. In Mirkwood Central South the Giant Spider was written down as more than seven times its real strength, so the game spent almost the whole budget summoning what it believed was the deadliest thing in the forest and was really the weakest -- and the Sword Spiders, Death Weepers and Black Spear Spiders behind it never got in. Dimholt was skipping the Thing of the Dark for the same reason.

    124 of those notes were wrong across the module, and 516 encounters were also recorded as "Very Easy" while actually set to Hard or Impossible -- Mirkwood Central South and Dimholt among them. All of it is corrected, and the build now refuses to complete if any of it drifts again. No creature was made stronger or weaker, and no XP or gold reward changed: the encounters simply pick the right monsters now.

  • Petrification not reliably giving respawn option; also seems to wait 3 minutes instead of expected 2
    DefectNeeds manual finishingRequested by: Szescian82
    Turning to stone still kills you after two minutes - and when it does you now get a death window whose Respawn button actually works. Click it and you get up at the bind point, the same as any other death, and it costs you nothing.

    What was happening: the game itself throws up a death window with the Respawn button greyed out the instant you turn to stone, and the client will not redraw a death window it is already showing - so two minutes later, when the timeout killed you and re-enabled the button, your screen never got the message. That is why logging out and back in 'fixed' it. The timeout now clears that stale window first and then kills you, so the panel you are looking at is a brand new one with a live button.

    An earlier attempt at this simply stood you back up automatically, with no window at all. That has been taken back out - dying and choosing to respawn is the normal way out, and it should look like every other death.

    The countdown is also two real minutes. It was running long - about 2:10, and longer on a busy server - because it counted server heartbeats and assumed each one was exactly 6 seconds. That is the 'waited 3 minutes' in the report; it was not your imagination. And the countdown messages themselves were printing twice; now they print once.

    Being cured with Stone to Flesh mid-countdown still cancels the whole thing.

    Also found on the way: respawning had been quietly destroying 10% of your gold - stock BioWare code that was never meant to be live here and that no recovery bag ever existed for. There is now no death penalty of any kind, on any respawn.
  • Tensor's Transformation not merging items reliably
    DefectNeeds manual finishingRequested by: Sync (Shync)
    8/24/26: Shapeshifting merges your gear every time now, not just when you happen to catch it. Tenser's Transformation, Polymorph Self, Shapechange, druid Wild Shape and Elemental Shape and every Shifter form share one merge, and it was being run before the game had finished putting you into the form - so on an unlucky cast there was no new hide or weapon to merge onto and nothing carried over. It now waits until the form's own hide and weapon are actually in your hands before it merges, so a cold cast with no buffs works the same as one after a full buff routine. Reported by Sync (Shync), who pinned down the tell: no buffs, no merge.

    It could also quietly duplicate the properties on your own weapon. When the merge ran too early it treated the weapon still in your hand as the form's weapon and copied that weapon's properties onto itself, permanently. That can no longer happen: the merge refuses to write to anything you were already carrying, and the “your equipment's magic flows into your new form” message now only appears when something really merged.


    8/26/26: LAST DEFECT related to tensors: all the weapon properties merge over and show on character sheet, EXCEPT "Enchantment Bonus". When I look at merged sword though, I see the Tensor's stock sword, and when examined I see it has it's original/default +3 Enchantment, and whatever enchantment bonus was on my unmorphed weapon, e.g. +15. 

    Since We get defects if a weapon as multiple enchantment bonuses, or if weapon has both an Attack bonus and Enchantment bonus, I think a well-rounded fix would be
    1. Check if morphed weapon has either a >= +4 or greater enchantment bonus or a >= +4 attack bonus property
    2. if so remove the default +3 enchantment bonus from Tensor's Sword.
    3. otherwise remove any enchantment/attack bonus property from the list of effects to morph into Tensor's Sword
    4. Assume player accessible (unmorphed) weapons wont have enchantment and attack bonus properites on this (this is build gated to prevent)

  • Gear to boost specific mage spells
    EnhancementNeeds manual finishingRequested by: dc0960 (Dungeon_Crawler)
    Original ask: "An item to improve epic summons would be nice, not to make them more tanks, but more dps so they do something vs higher bosses. Or a way to improve spells in general, obviously there are a lot of spells that can't be used as they are — mobs are just immune to them. Like an item to change implosion to do 'chaos' dmg instead of death. As a whole for casters there are very little ways to kill something, its either damage shields, IGMS or epic spell lucky rolls for the majority. So some boosting to lvl 9 spells would be nice."

    The problem. Against high-tier bosses casters hit a wall: bosses are immune to death/instant-kill and to whole swaths of spells, so the only reliable answers left are damage shields, IGMS, and lucky epic-spell rolls. There is no way to make a favourite spell — or your epic summons — actually matter at the top of the roster.

    Concept: caster gear that drops from high-tier bosses. Each piece is a single equippable that flags its wearer on equip; a scripted hook then reads that flag plus the spell being cast and adjusts the outcome. Four archetypes:
    • Spell / School Amplifier — multiplies a chosen spell's or school's damage by a capped % (e.g. "Evocation +25%", or a single named spell). Rides the same spell-id effect-scaling machinery the module already uses to double spell durations.
    • Damage Retyper — rewrites a spell's damage type to an existing type the target isn't immune to (e.g. Implosion's death damage → magical or negative), so immune bosses still take the hit. No new damage type is being invented here — this maps only onto existing NWN types; the separate forge "true damage type" idea is out of scope for this item.
    • Summoner's Gear — pushes a summon's attack/damage output (DPS, not tankiness — exactly as asked) up to where it matters against high-CR bosses, extending the existing summon-scaling that already boosts summons at spawn.
    • Focus / Caster-Level Gear — temporarily bumps caster level or save DC for a chosen school so save-based level-9 spells can actually land on high-CR targets. The trickiest to balance — treat as a stretch tier.
    Feasibility. No native item property can boost a specific spell, so all four are scripted — but the plumbing already exists in the module: tag-based equip markers, the spell-cast hook (the same one that runs the Meaningwave counterspell), the spell-id effect-scaling handler that doubles durations, and the summon-boost hook. Reuse, not new engine work.

    Open questions. One dedicated slot with hard % caps and no stacking of the same effect; which specific spells/schools ship first; and the drop sources / rarity across the boss roster.

    Starter catalogue (2026-07-09). A concrete first draft of 20 items — five each for Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer and Wizard is written up, with signature effects, damage dice / caster-level scaling, stat lines tiered to each source boss's challenge rating, and a thematic boss drop for every one (all existing, reachable bosses — no new areas needed). A new design pillar rewards pure single-class casters with a stronger version of every effect. See the full table on the wiki: Caster Signature Gear (linked from Customizations → Notable Custom Systems).

    Shipped 2026-08-05 -- the first piece of Caster Signature Gear is in the module: four Damage Retyper amulets, one for each caster class. Against high-tier bosses your best spell is often the one they are simply immune to; these turn a command to die into damage they actually have to survive. Reliquary of the Unmaking (Cleric) makes Implosion deal divine damage; Heartwood of the Blighted Grove (Druid) makes Finger of Death deal negative damage; Sorrow of the Nine (Sorcerer) makes Wail of the Banshee deal negative damage; Dreamshard of Dol Guldur (Wizard) makes Weird deal magical damage and drops its mind-affecting gate. All four are worn at the neck -- the one dedicated slot for this system, so only ever one retype is active and a second copy does not stack. Damage is one die per caster level (d6; d8 for Finger of Death), capped at 40 dice and 300 per target (400 for Finger of Death); metamagic and spell resistance still apply, and the target now rolls an ordinary Fortitude save for half instead of the death save an immune creature passes for free.   • Bard song issues round 2
    DefectShipped · Merit awardedAugust 21st, 2026Credit: Sync (Shync)

  • Shifter stats defect (stats go down after shifting)
    DefectShipped · Merit awardedAugust 20th, 2026Credit: Sync (Shync)
    Shifting now adds the shape's bonus to your own stats instead of replacing them. Every form used to overwrite your Strength, Constitution and Dexterity with the animal's own scores, so on this server a geared character came out of the change weaker than they went in -- less attack, less damage, less AC. Now you keep your own scores and gain what the shape is worth on top: a brown bear is +15 Strength / +7 Constitution / +1 Dexterity over whatever you already had, Dragon Shape is +36 / +20 / +24. Weak shapes stay weak (a badger still gives no Strength), but no shape can ever leave you worse off than unshifted.

    Your armour's own protection comes with you. Plate and shield stop being worn while shifted, and only their magic used to carry over -- the armour's base protection was simply lost. It is handed back on top of the form's natural armour now, so your AC while shifted is your normal AC plus what the shape adds.

    Claw and bite forms finally get your weapon's enhancement bonus. A +9 weapon merged into a shape with no hands was paying out its extra damage but none of its +9 to hit or to damage, which is why unarmed forms felt so far behind the ones that carry a weapon. That +9 now lands on every natural attack.

    All of it applies to druid Wild Shape and Elemental Shape, every Shifter shape, and to Polymorph Self, Shapechange and Tenser's Transformation. The bonuses end the moment the form does.

  • Weapons with both Attack Bonus (AB) and Enhancement Bonus (EB) appear to mis-calculate stats
    DefectShipped · Merit awardedJune 23rd, 2026Credit: Piskan (Alek Cain)
    Weapons carrying both an Attack Bonus and an Enhancement Bonus were misleading you: the NWN engine applies only the higher of the two to your attack roll, so any Attack Bonus at or below the Enhancement Bonus was dead weight on the character sheet. This is hardcoded engine behaviour with no toggle, so every weapon in the module was audited instead — 253 weapons had the two properties collapsed into a single Enhancement Bonus, raised to a balanced value where the old Attack Bonus was higher. Copies already in your inventory were repaired automatically.

    If you keep old copies in the Bank of Bree, take them out and leave them in a character's inventory overnight — the nightly 3am reboot scans character files, not storage boxes, so banked items will not be fixed until they are carried.
