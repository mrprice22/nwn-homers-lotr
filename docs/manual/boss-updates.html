Endgame Difficulty & New High-Level Tier

Top-end players have out-scaled the existing bosses and are asking for a real challenge. This page documents why the current bosses are easy for a maxed character, a concrete recipe for making them threatening again, and a design for opening three currently-inaccessible areas as a new highest-level tier populated with beefed-up "absent" creatures.

Status: design / proposal — not yet applied in-game. Nothing here is live. The numbers, placements, and links below are recommendations for review. When approved, apply per the implementation checklist and regenerate the boss registry.

Sources. Effective boss stats (AC/saves/SR/DR/immunities including gear) are in module-index/counter_gear.md; the boss list is module-index/bosses.json; the pool of unused creatures is module-index/unspawned_creatures.json; empty/unconnected areas are in module-index/area_index.json. Raw blueprints are unpacked/<resref>.utc.json.

Why the top end is too easy

The big CR numbers are cosmetic; two systemic holes make maxed players trivialise the fights.

Nearly every boss forces CRAdjust = 125 (the engine maximum), so the huge displayed Challenge Ratings (Theoden's Chosen 9540, Summanus 2938, …) are manual labels, not threat. Underneath, difficulty comes down to a hand-typed HP pool (5,000–13,000) plus inflated ability scores. counter_gear.md confirms zero current bosses are unbeatable with obtainable gear. Two weaknesses recur across the roster:

  1. Lopsided saves. The base save fields (fortbonus/refbonus/willbonus) are almost always 0; the big save numbers come purely from one inflated ability. Every boss therefore has a dumped stat that opens a save category wide to save-or-die / crowd control (Witch King Con 13 → Fort +3; Smaug Dex 10 → Ref +0; Gwathdor Wis 20 → Will +5; Summanus Wis 10 → Will +0).
  2. Flat "Soak" damage reduction is worthless. Players reach weapon enhancement +15 to +18, which bypasses all flat DR. Real defense on the good bosses comes from percentage damage-type immunity (the shared bossring = ~50% physical + magical) and from high effective AC (≥ 85 still forces misses against the maxed player attack ceiling of ~high +60s/+70s).

Player-power ceiling (what a boss must beat). Forgeable gear is capped at 6 properties / ~750k–1.25M gp value (forge_inc.nss). Enhancement and Attack-Bonus item properties do not stack for to-hit (the engine takes the max, not the sum), so a maxed melee to-hit from gear tops out near +18–20 over BAB/Str — realistic total AB in the high +60s/+70s. So: AC ≥ 85 stays relevant, every save ≥ ~+25 removes the cheese, and % immunity beats flat DR.

The tuning recipe

Per-boss, by hand — no shared script. Three levers, applied only where a boss has the hole.

  • Close the dumped save. Raise the relevant fortbonus/refbonus/willbonus so no save sits below ~+25. This is the single biggest anti-cheese fix.
  • Floor effective AC ≥ 85. Raise NaturalAC (cleanest, no gear conflicts) and/or add AC-bonus gear.
  • Ensure % immunity, not flat DR. If a boss lacks the shared invisible buff items, equip bossring (prop 20, ~50% physical + magical) and/or npcbuffgear (stacked resist + immunities) in its blueprint Equip_ItemList. Drop reliance on prop-23 "Soak" DR.

Leave HP and the CR label alone — the goal is tactical difficulty (fights that resist CC and force misses), not longer sponges.

Encounter density — double the CR>150 packs

More enemies per fight where it's a group encounter — not on solo-boss encounters.

Of the 36 encounters (unpacked/*.ute.json) whose pool contains a CR>150 creature, 22 are multi-creature packs (MaxCreatures > 1). For those, double both MaxCreatures (the cap) and RecCreatures (the number actually spawned) for ~2× enemies.

Do not double the 14 solo-boss encounters (MaxCreatures == 1) — e.g. angmar (CR 4260), iidda, moriabyss, gullom, understudy. New highest-level tier — three inaccessible areas

Opt-in zones off existing endgame hubs, populated with beefed-up "absent" creature blueprints.

The module has 15 finished, non-hidden areas with zero normal connections — cut off today. Three are thematically perfect for a top tier, span three different regions, and can each be reached from an existing endgame hub. Implementation checklist

When approved — the order of operations and the systems not to break.

  1. Boss tuning: edit the fortbonus/refbonus/willbonus/NaturalAC fields (and Equip_ItemList for immunity gear) directly in each unpacked/<resref>.utc.json.
  2. Encounter density: double MaxCreatures + RecCreatures on the 22 pack encounters only (never the 14 solo-boss encounters).
  3. Place new-tier bosses: in each area's .git.json Creature List, deep-copy a working sibling creature struct (keep __struct_id = 4), then overwrite only TemplateResRef, Tag, name, and position; add the matching .gic.json stub (__struct_id 4 + empty Comment). Never author from the .utc and never include Comment/PaletteID in the git struct. Use bin/place-helper.py <area> --suggest for coordinates. Verify FactionID is Hostile.
  4. Link the areas: add door/trigger transitions (+ return waypoints) in the source-hub .git.json files; for gated zones, put a key/plot check on the transition.
  5. Leashing is automatic for a boss cloned from a sibling with the standard x2_def_spawn OnSpawn (it records its "spawn" home; the area OnEnter leash_to_area keeps it home). Confirm the target area's OnEnter is leash_to_area or chains to it. Do not set NO_LEASH on a stationary boss.
  6. Regenerate the boss registry (any CR>60 single-instance placement auto-qualifies for the Roll of the Fallen board): python3 bin/gen-boss-registry.py (dry run) → --write → python3 tests/check_boss_registry.py. Never hand-edit the generated block in brd_db.nss. Confirm each new boss's OnDeath reaches SE_DoCreatureRespawn (e.g. x2_def_ondeath) so the board countdown is truthful.
  7. Build gates: a repack runs tests/smoke-test (boss registry, spawn-leash, forge, divergent-creatures, dialog-integrity). New qualifying content won't break them.

Do not break the wrapper chain. x2_def_spawn → nw_c2_default9 (stores the leash home; runs bst_install) → bst_install re-points OnDamaged/OnDeath to the bestiary trackers at runtime and chains the originals. A custom per-boss OnDamaged gimmick is preserved as the "original" and still runs — the bestiary, boss-tracker, and leash systems keep working.
