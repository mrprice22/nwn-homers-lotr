Custom Gameplay Items

Items that add mechanics the base game does not have, and items that work differently from stock.

Rod of Fast Buffing

Sold at the Well-Mart at the Well of Eru, the Rod of Fast Buffing lets casters batch their pre-combat buffs into a single action:

  • Store spells by casting buff spells at the rod — each one is remembered, in order.
  • Activate the rod (out of combat) to re-cast every stored spell on yourself, one after another, in one go.
  • The rod cannot be used in combat — it is a setup tool, not an in-fight panic button.

A big quality-of-life upgrade for buff-heavy casters who would otherwise click through a dozen spells before every fight. Based on the Rod of Fast Buffing prefab from the Neverwinter Vault.

Rod of Fast Lore

Also sold at the Well-Mart, the Rod of Fast Lore identifies a whole inventory at once instead of one item at a time:

  • Activate it on yourself (or on another player) to scan that character's inventory and attempt to identify every unidentified item in one pass.
  • Each item rolls a Lore check — your Lore skill + a d20 against a difficulty based on the item's value (gold value ÷ 100 + 1). Higher Lore identifies more, and more valuable items resist longer.
  • Your combat log lists what was identified and what failed (by item type), so you can re-try the failures after a Lore boost. When you scan another player, both of you see the results.

This is the same Lore rule the Party Loot Roll uses to identify unidentified drops.
