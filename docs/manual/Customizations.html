Customizations — Homer's LOTR

This module runs on stock Neverwinter Nights rules (plus CEP 2 content), but a number of spells, mechanics, and whole systems have been deliberately changed. These pages are the full reference for what has — and has not — been modified relative to a vanilla NWN game, drawn directly from the module's scripts. A short, in-game version lives in your journal under Server Info → Module Customizations.

Search below — it looks through the full text of every section, not just the titles — or browse by topic.

Attack Bonuses from Spells Stack

In stock NWN, attack bonuses from spells and effects don't add up: the game keeps only the single largest one. A permanent +5 from a feat would make a +2 from War Cry worth nothing at all.

Disarmed Weapons Go to Your Pack

When a monster disarms you, your weapon goes into your inventory instead of onto the ground. On the ground it could vanish when the area resets, or the monster could pick it up. Grab it and re-equip it.
